# H follow-up: lock-up terms and missing IPO terms for 26 listings

Researched 2026-08-21. Companion data file: `data/research/H/H1_lockup_terms.csv` (26 rows, 23 columns). Sources are the 424B4 prospectus on EDGAR (Underwriting / Lock-Up Agreements / Shares Eligible for Future Sale / Capitalization), closing 8-Ks, the first post-IPO 10-Q/10-K/20-F for greenshoe exercise and updated share counts, plus press for valuations, oversubscription and private rounds.

## Reading the CSV — conventions and caveats

- `lockup_days` is the contractual base period; `lockup_first_release` is the first date any meaningful locked block became tradable (day-1 employee windows count, e.g. ABNB, HOOD, PLTR); `lockup_full_release` is when the last large tranche unlocked (BABA/META/SPCX/FIG run to 12+ months). Where the prospectus keys the end to an earnings release, the date is computed from the dated earnings 8-K and flagged in `staged_provisions`.
- `float_pct = shares_sold_m / shares_out_post_m`. `shares_sold_m` includes greenshoe shares where the option was exercised *and* the row says so (CRCL, FIG, CRWV, ARM, CAVA, RIVN, HOOD, BMBL, ABNB, SNOW, ZM, BYND, BABA, META); RDDT's `shares_sold_m`/`float_pct` are base-deal (22.0M) while its `deal_size_usd_b` includes the 3.3M greenshoe. BMBL and other Up-C / dual-class structures are reported as-exchanged with the Class-A-only figure in the cell. COIN/PLTR (direct listings) have no "shares sold"; registered-for-resale counts are given instead.
- `ipo_val_usd_b`: basic (= shares outstanding x offer price) is always given; fully-diluted press figures are quoted alongside where found. `stepup_x` uses the basic figure unless stated.
- `oversub_x` and `retail_alloc_pct` are mostly `unavailable`: oversubscription is almost never disclosed in filings and the WebSearch quota for this session was exhausted early; where a directed-share-program reservation (%) is the only datum it is shown as such, not as actual retail allocation.
- Last private-round valuations are SECONDARY (press/Wikipedia) or ESTIMATE (derived from last-round price x as-converted shares in the prospectus) and are flagged per row. ARM (SoftBank/Vision Fund $64B internal transfer) and BIRK (L Catterton buyout) are not primary funding rounds.
- SPCX figures come from the in-repo 2026 filing set and carry the same caveats as the rest of the SPCX data in this repo.

## Headline non-standard features

- No lockup at all: COIN (direct listing). Partial day-1 tradability: PLTR (~29% of outstanding), ABNB (16.8M employee shares), HOOD (15% of employee holdings day 1, +15% day 91).
- Earnings-keyed expiries that lapsed early (< 180 days): CRCL (165 d), CRWV (140 d), RDDT (141 d), FIG (IPO-tier ~99 d after price trigger), HOOD (126-day base), ABNB (158 d), LYFT (143 d, blackout clause), PTON (151 d, blackout clause), PINS (day 180 but blackout-window clause).
- Price-trigger releases: FIG (met, 2025-09-05), SNOW (met, 2021-01-07), DASH (met, 2021-03-09), CART (35% employee tranche at > $36, apparently never met), HOOD founder/VC tranches (never triggered), SPCX (30% trigger tranche unconfirmed).
- Multi-tranche long tails: META (91/151-180/181/211/366 d), BABA (91/181/366 d), SPCX (20% at Q2-26 earnings, 7% tranches, extended lockup to Q2-2027 earnings, Musk 366 d), FIG company-level extended lockup (54% of shares) staged to 2026-08-31.
- Early waivers for follow-ons: BYND (3.25M sh at day 91), CRCL (11.5M sh for Aug-2025 follow-on).
- Greenshoe not (or only partly) exercised: CART, PTON, META, UBER (no), CRWV, HOOD, LYFT (partial); DASH had no over-allotment option at all; KLAR/BIRK exercise status not found.
- Priced below range: CRWV only. Down-round IPOs vs last private mark: CART (0.25x), RDDT (0.64x), PLTR (0.81x), ARM (0.85x), CRCL (0.90x), CRWV (~0.98x), PINS (1.03x).
